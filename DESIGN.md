# DESIGN.md — Direction for IN WEST TAMP

## Identity & Purpose
- **Brand**: Program Penguatan Kapasitas Organisasi Kemahasiswaan (PPK Ormawa) 2026
- **Ormawa**: Himpunan Mahasiswa Gizi (HIMAGI), Universitas Tidar (UNTIDAR), Magelang
- **Mitra**: Desa Tampirkulon, Kecamatan Candimulyo, Kabupaten Magelang (Wisata Air West Tamp Tubing)
- **Program**: IN WEST TAMP (Integrated Wellness-Tourism Area Tampirkulon)
- **Personality**: Grounded, authentic, community-driven, warm natural wellness, academically credible

## Dials (antislop)
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced, welcoming, official hospitality)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Consistent core layout with varied card compositions based on content needs)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Restrained micro-interactions on user tap/hover only, no floating loops or ambient glow churn)

## Palette (Nature Wellness)
- **Canvas / Ground**: `#F9F7F3` (Warm ivory sand, authentic natural feel)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp, solid card elevation with subtle warm borders)
- **Brand Primary (Deep Moss Forest)**: `#162E20` (High authority, deep natural contrast)
- **Brand Secondary (Earthy Pine)**: `#274C37`
- **Brand Accent (Warm Ochre / Gold)**: `#C87926` (Used deliberately on primary action & key badges only)
- **Text Primary**: `#112117` (Contrast ratio > 11:1 against white, WCAG AAA compliant)
- **Text Secondary**: `#455C4E` (Contrast ratio > 5.5:1 against canvas, WCAG AA compliant)
- **Border**: `rgba(22, 46, 32, 0.10)` (Subtle, tactile definition)

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Hierarchy**:
  - H1 / Hero: 1.85rem, 800 weight, -0.025em tracking
  - Section Headings: 1.15rem, 700 weight, -0.01em tracking
  - Card Titles: 1.05rem, 700 weight
  - Body Text: 0.92rem, 400-500 weight, 1.55 line-height

## Craftsmanship Rules Applied
1. **No Artificial Glow / Mesh Orbs**: Background is calm, solid, and warm natural ivory.
2. **Dose-Capped Glassmorphism**: Cards are solid, elevated with subtle shadow and crisp borders instead of blanket backdrop-blur.
3. **Intentional Radii**: Varied radii across cards (16px), buttons (10px), and badges (6px) to clearly differentiate component types.
4. **Content-Driven Composition**:
   - Drive Dokumentasi gets a distinct spotlight card with clear archival metadata.
   - Tiket Wisata gets a distinct destination card.
   - Social channels are organized cleanly.
   - 4 Pillars feature clean typographic hierarchy with bulleted actions.
